# From Artwork to Controlled Archive

**Public Systems Paper v1.0**

A control architecture for physical art and its digital descendants. The physical artwork remains the parent identity while scans, masters, products, transformations, certificates, licensing records, releases, and sale history remain traceable children of that source.
