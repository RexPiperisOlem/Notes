# AI Employment Radar

**Public Systems Paper v1.3**

A constraint-first decision architecture for finding AI-related work. It moves feasibility ahead of nuanced ranking, separates semantic fit from operational viability, maps consequential claims to evidence, and keeps applications and other consequential decisions human-approved.
