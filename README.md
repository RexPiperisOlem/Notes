# Published Documents

**A public shelf of finished long-form work and systems proof by Roger Crosby.**

This repository contains completed documents in artificial intelligence, cognition, operational systems, recipe-production methodology, systems design, and experimental literature. They are published here as inspectable evidence of finished work rather than as unsupported claims on a résumé.

The documents are not all the same kind of object. Some are Field Manuals, some are production systems, some are Systems Papers, some are speculative analysis, and some are literary experiments. What connects them is the working method:

- large or messy source material is given a defined container;
- claims are separated from metaphor and speculation;
- structure is used to make the material navigable;
- failure modes and control boundaries are made explicit when the subject requires them;
- the document is completed as an artifact rather than left as an idea;
- public work is separated from the private production archive.

## Systems Papers

The [Systems Papers](systems-papers/) are a separate public document family. They explain systems that were actually built, used, tested, revised, or recovered without publishing the full internal machinery that operates them.

The purpose is **proof, not a consulting giveaway**. A Systems Paper should make the architecture legible enough that another person can understand the problem, the design decisions, the control logic, the failure modes, and the limits. It should not function as a complete reproduction kit for the private operating system behind the paper.

### [Systems Paper 001 — Rebuilding the Room](systems-papers/001-rebuilding-the-room/)

A public proof-of-work paper about recovering preferred large-language-model interaction behaviour through external operating controls rather than model-weight modification.

The paper covers the problem created when model behaviour changes across versions, the distinction between model capability and operating behaviour, the role of state discipline and external control documents, drift and failure categories, calibration, human authority, and the boundary between public architectural proof and private implementation detail.

**Demonstrates:** AI workflow architecture, behavioural continuity, external control systems, model-agnostic documentation, failure analysis, calibration thinking, public/private boundary design, and technical systems writing.

### [Systems Paper 002 — From Job Search to Employment Intelligence](systems-papers/002-ai-employment-radar/)

A public proof-of-work paper about converting a noisy AI job search into a constraint-first employment-intelligence system.

The underlying private system was developed through sequential prototypes, beginning with a deliberately mixed batch of **30 live job listings**. The public edition shows the architecture and development logic without publishing the private scorecard, candidate constraints, detailed search configuration, test records, or operating Bible.

The paper covers feasibility before relevance, the separation of content fit from employment fit, title-vocabulary discovery, evidence matching, truth-preserving translation, human-gated consequential action, failure-to-infrastructure learning, and the rule that a system should earn complexity through low-cost testing before formalization.

**Demonstrates:** constraint-first decision architecture, AI-assisted evaluation, human oversight, evidence-backed capability translation, sequential prototyping, failure analysis, knowledge and vocabulary learning, claims discipline, and public/private boundary design.

## Document guide

### [The Dark Cognitive Manual](THE%20DARK%20COGNITIVE%20MANUAL.pdf)

A completed public Field Manual about cognition, interpretation, pressure, uncertainty, attention, motivation, and the stories people use to explain their own behaviour.

It is not presented as clinical psychology, diagnosis, therapy, or a scientific textbook. It is an operational document: a structured set of observations and working distinctions intended to help a reader notice how thought and behaviour fail under real conditions.

The document demonstrates:

- turning broad behavioural material into a navigable manual;
- maintaining a consistent field-reference voice;
- separating observation from instruction;
- using headings, rules, and repeated forms to control a large subject;
- completing a substantial practical nonfiction artifact.

### [Sixty AI Futures](Sixty%20AI%20Futures.pdf)

Sixty compressed future scenarios examining artificial intelligence as infrastructure, institution, collaborator, labour system, commercial force, threat surface, cultural pressure, and ordinary tool.

The collection does not claim that all sixty futures will occur. Its value is range and pressure testing: each scenario changes the conditions and asks what becomes possible, likely, profitable, dangerous, invisible, or normal.

The document demonstrates:

- sustained high-volume conceptual production without collapsing into one repeated prediction;
- the ability to vary scale, tone, stakeholders, and consequences;
- systems thinking across technology, institutions, culture, work, and human behaviour;
- disciplined speculative writing that preserves uncertainty;
- conversion of long-form discussion into a finished public artifact.

### [The Recipe Writing Production Guide](THE%20RECIPE%20WRITING%20PRODUCTION%20GUIDE.pdf)

A practical production system for writing recipes that survive contact with real kitchens.

The guide treats a recipe as an operating document rather than decorative food copy. It addresses sequencing, ingredient identity, substitutions, timing, equipment, yields, ambiguity, human variation, quality checks, and the kinds of omissions that make a recipe technically attractive but operationally useless.

The document grows out of more than twenty years of food-service and hospitality experience and demonstrates:

- converting tacit professional judgment into explicit procedure;
- writing for operators under time and information constraints;
- anticipating failure points before publication;
- documentation architecture, production standards, and quality assurance;
- translation between subject-matter expertise and usable instructions.

### A Zoo at the End of Winter

A completed long-form experimental literary work built through a cold institutional and ledger-like voice.

The work uses recurring animals, structures, rituals, and winter systems to examine behaviour before explanation. It is not a conventional novel, devotional work, or simple satire. It uses literary form as an observing instrument.

Available formats:

- [Read the PDF](A%20Zoo%20at%20the%20End%20of%20Winter%20032026.pdf)
- [Download the Word document](A%20Zoo%20at%20the%20End%20of%20Winter%20032026.docx)

The document demonstrates:

- maintaining a distinct voice across a substantial manuscript;
- building a symbolic system without explaining every symbol;
- organizing recurring patterns across a long work;
- carrying an unconventional idea through to a finished document;
- literary range alongside operational and technical writing.

### [Character Study](Character%20Study.pdf)

A public creative document from the earlier archive.

It is included as evidence of development and range rather than presented as the current flagship. The document shows an earlier approach to character observation, voice, and construction and provides useful contrast with the more controlled systems and long-form work produced later.

## Public and private boundary

This repository contains finished public artifacts. It does not contain:

- the complete private source archive;
- raw conversations and personal records;
- internal operating Bibles;
- complete private implementation recipes behind the Systems Papers;
- unreleased manuscripts;
- private strategy or commercial records;
- credentials, platform configuration, or customer data.

A public Systems Paper may describe a private system's architecture, purpose, failure modes, and lessons. It does not imply that the complete operating documents, control prompts, calibration material, client method, or source chain are public.

The presence of a finished public document does not imply that all source material or production methods are public.
